# QuantumCoin Founder Vesting Policy

Status: QC_FOUNDER_VESTING_POLICY_V1

Date: 20260619_204927

## Purpose

This step defines founder allocation and vesting rules.

This step does not execute unlock, mint QC, change balances, change consensus, or change max supply.

## Founder allocation

- Max supply: 25,500,000 QC
- Founder total: 1,275,000 QC
- Founder total percent: 5%
- Founder liquid/open: 255,000 QC
- Founder liquid/open percent: 1%
- Founder locked: 1,020,000 QC
- Founder locked percent: 4%

Founder allocation comes only from fixed supply. It is not new minting.

## Vesting schedule

- Cliff: 12 months
- Vesting: 48 months
- Total schedule: 60 months
- Unlock starts: month 13
- Unlock ends: month 60
- Monthly unlock: 21,250 QC
- 48 x 21,250 QC = 1,020,000 QC

## Wallet rule

- Founder wallet must be finalized before mainnet.
- Founder wallet must not change after mainnet.

## Forbidden actions

The following cannot unlock or change founder vesting:

- AI
- admin panel automatic action
- worker
- Telegram Game
- TGWT
- NFT system
- Watch & Earn

## Safe boundary

This step does not:

- mint QC
- change max supply
- change balances
- send funds
- execute unlock
- change consensus
- change mining reward
- start or stop miners
