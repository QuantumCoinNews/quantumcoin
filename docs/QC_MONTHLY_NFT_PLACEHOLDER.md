## Decision

QuantumCoin monthly NFT rewards are prepared before mainnet and distributed only after final/mainnet activation.

## Rules

- 1 rare NFT per month.
- 12 rare NFTs per year.
- Every monthly NFT must be independent and unique.
- No NFT may be minted in testnet placeholder mode.
- Monthly winner selection is placeholder/audit-only until mainnet.
- Winner must pass eligibility, AI review, and admin approval.
- Mainnet mint requires explicit manual approval.
- NFT reward must not change QC supply.
- NFT reward must not change QC balance.
- Final artwork and final metadata must be prepared before mainnet distribution.

## Current scope

This step does not mint NFT, does not write chain metadata, does not change balances, and does not start the monthly NFT reward program on mainnet.
